---
title: "Troubleshooting During an Exam"
description: "Causes and fixes for common participant problems: cannot sign in, registration and payment, a test that will not start, problems during a test, and scores that do not appear yet."
---

This page collects the problems participants run into most often and how to solve them. App messages are written in **bold** exactly as they appear, so you can match them with the message on your screen.

<Info>
  If the problem is not solved, contact your exam organiser. Include your full name, account email, exam name, participant code (see **Kartu Peserta**, the participant card), when it happened, and a screenshot of the message.
</Info>

## Signing in and accounts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The message Alamat email atau kata sandi tidak valid appears">
    **Alamat email atau kata sandi tidak valid** means invalid email or password. Check in order:

    1. **The address is right.** Make sure you opened your organiser's participant app address, for example `https://prestasi.indolat.web.id`. Accounts from another organiser cannot be used.
    2. **The email and password are right.** Passwords are case-sensitive. Click the eye icon to show what you typed.
    3. **Your account is active.** The same message appears if the organiser has deactivated your account.

    If you forgot your password, see [Forgotten password](/en/peserta/membuat-akun-dan-masuk#forgotten-password).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message Terlalu banyak percobaan masuk appears">
    **Terlalu banyak percobaan masuk** means too many sign-in attempts. After 5 failed attempts within one minute, you have to wait a little. The message tells you how many seconds to wait. Wait, then sign in with the right email and password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The email verification code does not arrive">
    - Check the spam, promotions, or social folders of your email. The subject is **Kode OTP Anda: …** (your OTP code).
    - The code is valid for 5 minutes. If **Kirim Ulang Kode** (resend code) shows **OTP sebelumnya masih berlaku.** (the previous OTP is still valid), wait a few minutes, then click it again.
    - Make sure the email you registered is right. If you mistyped it, ask the organiser to correct it or register again with the right email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message Sesi Anda telah kedaluwarsa, silakan masuk kembali. appears">
    Your session has expired, for example after a long time without activity. Sign in again. If a test is in progress, it opens again straight away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message Akun Anda telah dinonaktifkan. Silakan hubungi administrator. appears">
    The organiser has deactivated your account. Contact the organiser to reactivate it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A sign-in link from the organiser does not open">
    If **Tautan masuk sudah kedaluwarsa atau sudah digunakan. Minta tautan baru dari penyelenggara.** appears, the link can only be used once or has expired. Ask the organiser for a new link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Registration and payment

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There is no Pendaftaran Tes Baru button">
    The **Pendaftaran Tes Baru** (new test registration) button only appears when an exam is open for registration. If the organiser registered you directly, your tests are already on **Daftar Tes** (test list) and you do not need to register.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An exam is labelled Kuota Penuh">
    **Kuota Penuh** means the registration quota is full. When you register, the message **Kuota tes sudah penuh, silakan hubungi administrator untuk mendapatkan kuota tambahan** appears. Contact the organiser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The registration shows Proses Verifikasi">
    **Proses Verifikasi** means the organiser is checking your registration data first. The test opens once the registration is approved. If it takes too long, contact the organiser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have paid, but the status still shows Menunggu Pembayaran">
    Reload the **Detail Pembayaran** (payment details) page a little while after paying. If the status has not changed to **Sudah Dibayar** (paid), contact the organiser with your proof of payment (exam name, amount, and time of payment).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message Waktu pembayaran telah habis. appears">
    The 2-hour payment deadline has passed, so the QR code can no longer be used. Register again to get a new QR code, or contact the organiser. See [Paying for a paid exam](/en/peserta/mendaftar-tes#paying-for-a-paid-exam).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Test will not start

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Kerjakan Tes button cannot be clicked or shows Terkunci">
    Possible causes:

    - The session has not started yet or has already ended. Check **Waktu Pengerjaan** (test window) on the test card.
    - The tests must be taken in order and an earlier test is not finished yet.
    - The organiser has not placed you in a session.
    - The test is still waiting for payment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A message appears when clicking Kerjakan Tes or Mulai Tes">
    | Message | Meaning |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Tes ini hanya dapat dimulai pada jadwal sesinya.** | Wait until your session starts. |
    | **Anda belum dijadwalkan pada sesi tes ini. Silakan hubungi guru atau admin ujian.** | The organiser has not placed you in a session. |
    | **Tes ini masih terkunci. Selesaikan tes sebelumnya terlebih dahulu atau tunggu hingga tes ini dibuka.** | Finish the earlier test first. |
    | **Tes tidak dapat dimulai karena lalu lintas saat ini penuh. Silakan coba lagi nanti atau hubungi administrator.** | The limit on simultaneous participants has been reached. Try again a little later. |
    | **Kuota tes tidak mencukupi. Silakan hubungi administrator untuk informasi lebih lanjut.** | A quota problem on the organiser's side. Contact the organiser. |
    | **Ujian ini mewajibkan berbagi layar. Silakan unduh dan gunakan aplikasi CBT Indolat untuk mengerjakan ujian.** | Take the exam in the CBT Indolat app. |
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Kamera check does not pass">
    If **Tes tidak dapat dimulai. Silakan izinkan akses kamera untuk melanjutkan.** (allow camera access to continue) appears:

    1. Allow camera access for this site. In Google Chrome, click the icon to the left of the site address, then turn on **Camera**.
    2. Close other apps that are using the camera, such as video meeting apps.
    3. Reload the page, then click **Kerjakan Tes** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message Browser Tidak Didukung appears">
    **Browser Tidak Didukung** means the browser is not supported. Use an up-to-date Google Chrome, Chromium, Microsoft Edge, or Safari, or the CBT Indolat app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Problems during the test

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The indicator changes to Koneksi Buruk">
    **Koneksi Buruk** (poor connection) means your device has lost its internet connection. Answers already saved are not lost. Once the connection is back, the indicator returns to **Koneksi Baik** (good connection). Reload the page if asked, then check **Daftar Soal** (question list) to make sure your last answer is green.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The browser closed or the device switched off">
    Open the participant app address again and sign in. The test in progress opens straight away with your previous answers. The time remaining keeps going down while the browser is closed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message Sesi tidak valid. Akun Anda digunakan di perangkat lain. appears">
    Your account was used to sign in on another device. An account can only be active on one device. Continue the test on the device that signed in last. If that was not you, change your password and contact the organiser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A Peringatan window about switching tabs appears">
    The exam detected a tab switch. Click **Konfirmasi** (confirm), then stay on the test page. Every switch is recorded, and the organiser may stop your test if violations continue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message Tes Ditangguhkan appears">
    **Tes Ditangguhkan** means the organiser has paused your test. Wait until **Ujian Dilanjutkan** (exam resumed) appears. The pause does not reduce your time remaining, unless the session ends first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message Tes Dihentikan or Tes Direset appears">
    **Tes Dihentikan** means the organiser has ended your test. **Tes Direset** means your answers were deleted and the test can be taken again from the start. Go back to **Daftar Tes** and contact the organiser if you do not know why.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message Terjadi Kesalahan appears">
    **Terjadi Kesalahan** means an error occurred. Click **Muat Ulang Halaman** (reload page). Answers already saved are not lost. If the message keeps appearing, contact the organiser.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## After the test

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My score does not appear in Riwayat Tes">
    Possible causes: the organiser does not show scores, the essay test has not been marked yet, or the IRT score has not been calculated yet. See [Test history](/en/peserta/melihat-hasil#test-history).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There is no Lihat Hasil Tes button">
    The organiser does not show the answer sheet for that test.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There is no Unduh Sertifikat button">
    The exam has no certificate, or you have not met its requirements yet, for example you have not finished every test or your score is below the minimum. See [Certificate](/en/peserta/melihat-hasil#certificate).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My name is not on the leaderboard yet">
    Leaderboards are not refreshed automatically. Your name appears after you finish the test and the organiser refreshes the leaderboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
