This glossary lists terms commonly used in CBT Indolat. The explanations are kept simple to help you understand what each term does without needing any technical knowledge.

## A. Exams & Participants

### CBT

**Meaning:** An exam taken on a computer, laptop, tablet or phone (CBT stands for computer-based test).

**Example:**<br />Participants take an SNBT tryout (a practice exam for Indonesia's national university entrance test) online through CBT Indolat.

---

### Exam Session (Sesi Ujian)

**Meaning:** The schedule or time slot in which participants take an exam.

**Example:**<br />The tryout is split into a **Morning Session at 08:00** and an **Afternoon Session at 13:00**.

---

### Multi-Level

**Meaning:** An exam with several stages or levels that participants must go through in order.

**Example:**<br />A competition has **Level 1 Preliminary Round**, **Level 2 Semifinal** and **Level 3 Final**. Participants who pass Level 1 can move on to Level 2.

---

### Participant Verification (Verifikasi Peserta)

**Meaning:** The process that confirms a participant can move on to the next exam level.

**Example:**<br />After a participant is declared to have passed Level 1, the admin verifies them so they can take Level 2.

---

### Final Score (Skor Akhir)

**Meaning:** The final score a participant gets after taking an exam.

**Example:**<br />A participant gets a **Final Score of 85** in a maths tryout.

---

### Exam History (Riwayat Tes)

**Meaning:** The page where participants see the exams they've taken and their results.

**Example:**<br />A participant opens the **Exam History** menu to see their scores and download certificates.

---

## B. Questions & Question Packages

### Question Package (Paket Soal)

**Meaning:** A set of questions and answers used in an exam.

**Example:**<br />The admin creates one package with **50 maths questions** for an SNBT tryout.

---

### Question Package Template (Template Paket Soal)

**Meaning:** A ready-made question package you can use as a starting point.

**Example:**<br />The admin uses the **Latihan Try Out** (tryout practice) template, which already contains 10 questions, and then adapts it.

---

### Import Questions (Import Soal)

**Meaning:** A way to add many questions at once from an Excel file.

**Example:**<br />The admin already has 100 questions in Excel, so they don't need to enter them one by one.

---

### Generate Questions (Jasper AI)

**Meaning:** The **Jasper AI** feature, which creates questions and answers automatically from the instructions you give. In the app, you open it with the **Create a Question Package with Jasper AI** button.

**Example:**<br />The admin asks Jasper AI to create **10 medium-difficulty maths questions**.

---

### Question Group (Kelompok Soal)

**Meaning:** A way of grouping questions by topic or category.

**Example:**<br />Questions are split into the groups **Mathematics**, **Indonesian** and **English**.

---

### Multiple Choice (Pilihan Ganda)

**Meaning:** A question with several answer options, where the participant picks the one correct answer.

**Example:**<br />The participant chooses answer A, B, C, D or E.

---

### Complex Multiple Response (Majemuk Kompleks)

**Meaning:** A question with several statements, where the participant picks one of two labels for each statement, such as True/False or Fact/Opinion.

**Example:**<br />The statement **“The reading club meets every week”** is marked **Fact**, while **“The reading club is the most fun activity”** is marked **Opinion**.

---

### Passage (Wacana)

**Meaning:** A reading text, image or audio shared by several questions and shown as part of each of those questions during the exam, right above the question text.

**Example:**<br />One text about reading club attendance is used by three literacy questions, so participants only need to read it once.

---

### Short Answer (Jawaban Singkat)

**Meaning:** A question answered by typing a number or a short text. A question can have more than one accepted answer. Previously called Input Numerik (Numeric Input).

**Example:**<br />The question **“What is 10 \+ 15?”** is answered with **25**, and the question **“What is the capital of Indonesia?”** can be answered **DKI Jakarta** or **Jakarta**.

---

### Shuffle Questions (Acak Soal)

**Meaning:** A setting that lets the question order differ for each participant.

**Example:**<br />Question 1 for participant A may appear as question 5 for participant B.

---

### Shuffle Answers (Acak Jawaban)

**Meaning:** A setting that lets the order of the answer options differ for each participant.

**Example:**<br />Answer options A–B–C–D for participant A may appear in a different order for participant B.

---

## C. Users & Access

### Admin Role (Role Admin)

**Meaning:** A user role that determines which menus someone can open and manage.

**Example:**<br />**Admin Soal** (question admin) only manages question packages, while **Admin Ujian** (exam admin) manages exams and sessions.

---

### Admin Team (Tim Admin)

**Meaning:** Other members who are given access to help manage the CBT.

**Example:**<br />The admin invites a teacher as **Admin Soal** so they can help prepare questions.

---

## D. Domains

### Custom Domain (Kustom Domain)

**Meaning:** A dedicated address for opening the CBT, using a name that matches the institution.

**Example:**<br />Participants can open the CBT at:

> `tryout.lembagaku.com`

so the address is easier to recognise.

---

### External Domain (Domain Eksternal)

**Meaning:** A domain the institution already owns and then connects to CBT Indolat.

**Example:**<br />A school already owns `ujian.sekolahku.sch.id` and wants to use it for its CBT.

---

## E. Certificates

### Exam Certificate (Sertifikat Ujian)

**Meaning:** A certificate given to participants who have taken an exam and meet the requirements.

**Example:**<br />Participants who complete the tryout with a score of at least 70 get a certificate.

---

### Template Status (Published/Draft)

**Meaning:** The certificate's **Status** setting, which determines whether participants can use the certificate.

**Example:**<br />While the certificate's status is still **Draft**, participants can't request or download it. Change the status to **Published** so the certificate can be used.

---

### Design Template (Template Desain)

**Meaning:** The visual style used for the certificate.

**Example:**<br />The admin chooses **Template A**, **Template B**, or their own design through **Custom Template**.

---

### Minimum Score (Skor Minimum)

**Meaning:** The lowest score a participant must get to receive the certificate.

**Example:**<br />If the Minimum Score is **70**, a participant who scores 65 can't get the certificate yet.

---

### The exam must be completed (Wajib selesai ujian)

**Meaning:** A setting that requires participants to finish the exam before they get the certificate.

**Example:**<br />A participant already has a score of 80 but still has an unfinished exam, so the certificate can't be issued yet.

---

### Needs manual review (Butuh review manual)

**Meaning:** A setting that requires the admin to check and approve the certificate first.

**Example:**<br />The participant clicks **Request Certificate** and then waits for the admin's approval.

---

### Signatory (Penandatangan)

**Meaning:** The person whose name, position and signature appear on the certificate.

**Example:**<br />The certificate is signed by the **Institution Director**.

---

### Certificate Designer (Desainer)

**Meaning:** The section for arranging the certificate's layout and the position of its text.

**Example:**<br />The admin moves the participant's name to the centre and enlarges the certificate title.

---

### Request Certificate (Ajukan Sertifikat)

**Meaning:** The button participants use to request a certificate that needs the admin's approval.

**Example:**<br />A participant who meets the requirements clicks **Request Certificate**.

---

### Waiting for Approval (Menunggu Persetujuan)

**Meaning:** Shows that the certificate request is still waiting for the admin to review it.

**Example:**<br />After requesting a certificate, the participant sees the status **Waiting for Approval**.

---

### Download Certificate (Unduh Sertifikat)

**Meaning:** The button for getting a certificate that's ready as a PDF.

**Example:**<br />Once the request is approved, the participant selects **Download Certificate**.

---

## F. Finance

### Transaction History (Riwayat Transaksi)

**Meaning:** The menu for viewing payments made by participants.

**Example:**<br />The admin sees a **Rp50,000** payment from a participant to take a tryout.

---

### Transaction No. (No. Transaksi)

**Meaning:** A unique number that distinguishes one payment from another.

**Example:**<br />The admin uses the transaction number to find a specific payment.

---

### Successful Transaction (Sukses)

**Meaning:** The participant's payment has been received.

**Example:**<br />Once the payment is received, the status changes to **Success**.

---

### Expired Transaction (Kedaluwarsa)

**Meaning:** The payment wasn't completed before the deadline.

**Example:**<br />A participant creates a transaction but doesn't pay before the payment time runs out.

---

### Withdrawable Balance (Saldo Dapat Ditarik)

**Meaning:** Balance that can already be transferred to the institution's bank account.

**Example:**<br />A Withdrawable Balance of **Rp5,000,000** can be requested for payout.

---

### Pending Balance (Saldo Tertunda)

**Meaning:** Balance that has come in but can't be paid out yet.

**Example:**<br />A participant's newly received payment is still in the **Pending Balance**.

---

### Balance Withdrawal (Penarikan Saldo)

**Meaning:** The process of moving balance from CBT Indolat to the institution's bank account.

**Example:**<br />The admin withdraws **Rp3,000,000** to the institution's BCA account.

---

### Bank Account Details (Informasi Rekening)

**Meaning:** The bank account details used to receive balance withdrawals.

These include the **Bank Name, Account Number and Account Holder Name**.

**Example:**<br />The admin enters the institution's BCA account before withdrawing the balance.

---

### Withdrawal Status (Status Penarikan)

**Meaning:** Shows the progress of a balance withdrawal.

**Example:**

- **Pending** → the withdrawal is still being processed.
- **Approved** → the withdrawal has been processed to the destination account.
